# Brain downloads

Download Brain for an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or later and connect it to Google Meet in Chrome.

The Mac app is Developer ID signed, notarized and stapled. The Chrome companion is available from the Chrome Web Store. Meeting recording requires explicit opt-in and macOS microphone and screen/system-audio permissions.

## Verification

The direct Mac 0.5.22 (41) ZIP passed strict signature, notarization-ticket, extracted-archive, Gatekeeper and distribution checks with a quarantine marker. Its anonymous public download matched the verified artifact. The black Nunito Black 900 B and recording dot use a licensed shared outline. Copyright and SIL OFL 1.1 notices are included in the app resources. Real-meeting acceptance for this exact direct ZIP remains open.

Automatic connection supports Chrome companion 0.1.8 and later. Companion 0.1.9 carries the matching black-and-white branding. Mac 0.5.22 (41) is available in the existing Personal Devices internal TestFlight group under Brain: Meetings Transcription. Its public App Store submission is awaiting Apple review. Chrome companion 0.1.9 was published on November 12, 2026. Its version and black-and-white store icon were verified on the public listing.

Whisper transcription runs entirely offline on the Mac. Brain never uploads audio or transcripts. A folder provider can synchronize Markdown notes when you choose a synchronized destination.
